What is this study about?

We are studying how students use an AI study assistant in a university learning portal, and how the way an answer is presented affects understanding. The results will help design learning tools that adapt to the people using them.

What will I do?

You will ask the study assistant five questions on different subjects (for example biology or economics) and read its answers. After each answer you rate it and answer one short question about it. The way an answer is shown may change while you read it.

What is recorded?

  • Your webcam is used to estimate your facial expression and where you look on the screen. The video is analysed live and never saved; only numbers (for example "neutral, 82 %") are stored.
  • Your clicks, mouse movements, scrolling and on-screen answers.

Are there any risks?

None beyond normal computer use. You can stop at any time; just tell the researcher.

What happens to my data?

Your data is stored under a participant code, never your name. It will be used for research and to train computer models, and may appear in anonymised, group-level form in publications. You can ask for your data to be deleted at the end of the session, or later by quoting your participant code.
