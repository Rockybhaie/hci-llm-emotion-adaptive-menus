Thank you for taking part. We are developing a study assistant whose answers adapt to how the student feels.

While you read, the webcam estimated your facial expression. When it looked like you might be confused or unhappy for a few seconds, the assistant changed how the answer was shown (for example from paragraphs to a chart) and asked whether to keep the new view. The answers themselves were prepared in advance, so every participant saw the same content.

Your ratings and choices tell us which kind of presentation helps students most in which state. A future version will use this to adapt answers automatically. Nothing you did measures your ability.
