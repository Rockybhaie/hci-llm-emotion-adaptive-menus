Thank you for taking part. We are developing websites whose menus adapt to how the user is feeling. To do that, a computer model must first learn what different feelings look like in eye movements, skin conductance, heart rate and mouse behaviour.

Some parts of this session were therefore designed to bring on a particular feeling for a short time. We could not tell you this at the start, because knowing it changes how people react.

Nothing you did reflects your ability. The feelings these activities create normally fade within minutes. If anything is still bothering you, please tell the researcher, who can point you to university support services.
