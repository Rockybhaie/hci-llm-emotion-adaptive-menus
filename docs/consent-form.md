## What is this study about?

We are studying how people find their way around websites, and how the body responds while they do so. The results will help design websites that adapt to the people using them.

## What will I do?

The session takes about 40 minutes (about 1 hour including setup). You will:

- watch short videos or do short activities,
- use a website to find information,
- answer brief questions about how you feel.

Some parts are designed to influence how you feel, for example by being challenging, repetitive or emotional. Everything will be fully explained at the end.

## What is recorded?

- Where you look on the screen, and your pupil size (eye tracker under the monitor)
- Skin conductance, from two small sensors on two fingers of one hand
- Heart rate, from a chest strap or finger sensor
- Your clicks, mouse movements and on-screen answers

**No video or audio of you is recorded.**

## Are there any risks?

Some activities may feel mildly stressful, frustrating or boring, and some clips may make you feel sad or amused. These feelings are brief. You may stop at any time without giving a reason, and without any penalty.

## What happens to my data?

- Your data is stored under a participant code, never with your name.
- It is kept on a secure university computer and used for research and to train computer models.
- Results are published only in anonymised, group-level form.
- You can withdraw your data at the end of the session, or later by contacting the researchers and quoting your code: **P____ / Group ____**.
