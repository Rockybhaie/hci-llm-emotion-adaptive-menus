## What is this study about?

We are studying how students use an AI study assistant in a university learning portal, and how the way an answer is
presented affects understanding. The results will help design learning tools that adapt to the people using them.

## What will I do?

About 15 minutes. You will ask the study assistant five questions on different subjects (for example biology or economics) and read its answers. After each answer
you rate it and answer one short question about it. The way an answer is shown may change while you read it.

## What is recorded?

- Your **webcam** is used to estimate your facial expression and where you look on the screen. The video is analysed live and
  **never saved**; only numbers (for example "neutral, 82 %") are stored.
- Your clicks, mouse movements, scrolling and on-screen answers.

## Are there any risks?

None beyond normal computer use. You may stop at any time without giving a reason.

## What happens to my data?

Stored under a participant code, never your name, on a secure university computer; used for research and to train computer
models; published only in anonymised, group-level form. You can withdraw your data at the end of the session or later by
quoting your code: **P____**.
