# Debrief (researcher script)

The on-screen debrief already explains the purpose of the study and the exact manipulations this participant experienced. After it, give a short verbal debrief. Use these points in your own words.

1. **Purpose.** "We're building websites whose menus adapt to how the user feels. To do that, a model first has to learn what different feelings look like in eye movements, skin conductance, heart rate and mouse behaviour."
2. **Why we couldn't tell you.** "If people know a task is meant to frustrate or bore them, they react differently, so we explained this only at the end."
3. **What was deliberate.** Say only what applies to the participant's group:
   - **G1:** the arithmetic task was built to be too hard, and the "average" marker and the comparison with other participants were not real.
   - **G2:** the lag, ignored clicks, closing menus and "session expired" messages were deliberate, there was no real speed bonus, and the copying task was meant to be dull.
   - **G3:** the clips were chosen to bring on particular moods, and the bonus points were not linked to a real prize.
4. **Nothing reflects ability.** "Nothing you did measures your ability. Everyone gets the same difficulties."
5. **Wellbeing check.** "How are you feeling now?" If the participant is still upset, take time, chat about something neutral, and give them the university support contact: [counselling service contact].
6. **Confidentiality.** "Please don't tell other people what happens in the study. It would change their results."
7. **Withdrawal.** Remind them they can still withdraw later using their participant code.

| Group | Emotions | Manipulations to explain |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | Stress, Calm | MIST arithmetic, fake average, evaluation banner, countdown |
| G2 | Frustration, Boredom | Website sabotage, speed bonus, copying task, repetitive tasks |
| G3 | Joy, Sadness | Film clips, bonus points |
