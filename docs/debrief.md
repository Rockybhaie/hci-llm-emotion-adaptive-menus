# Debrief (researcher script)

The on-screen debrief explains the study and asks again for permission to keep the data. Afterwards, say in your own words:

1. **Purpose.** "We're building a study assistant whose answers adapt to how the student feels. First we need to learn which
   way of showing an answer helps in which state."
2. **What happened.** "The webcam estimated your facial expression. When you looked confused or unhappy for a few seconds, the
   assistant changed how the answer was shown and asked whether to keep it. The answers were prepared in advance, so everyone
   saw the same content."
3. **Not a test.** "The quick questions weren't grading you; they tell us which presentation helps people understand."
4. **Confidentiality.** "Please don't tell others that the answers change with your expression; it would change their results."
5. **Withdrawal.** They can still withdraw later by quoting their participant code.
